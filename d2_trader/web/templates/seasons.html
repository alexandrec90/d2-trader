How resets change prices here

Launch, first 14 days
Ladder prices start high and fall as supply grows. The range uses only the last 3 days of this season.
Season ending, last 21 days
Ladder prices slide. Notifications say so, so a low price is not mistaken for a bargain that lasts.
Between seasons
Ladder markets are closed: no ranges, and alerts pause. Non-ladder windows restart, because ladder items have just arrived there.
Always
A ladder range never includes a sale from an earlier season.
