Where the data comes from

diablo2.io
Item catalog, plus the last 10 sales per item, lagged 3 days. Automatic, within its robots.txt, once a day.

Live asks, bids and completed trades per market. Off by default because its Terms of Service forbid automated access. See the README before setting TRADERIE_ENABLED.
d2jsp
Paste only, on the Import page. Its rules forbid automation.
Blizzard news
The official season schedule, checked every few hours, and cross-checked against diablo2.io's announcement.
