Every fg price of a rune is one reading: the rune's value in Ist from Traderie trades, over the fg it went for. All readings feed one model of the rate as a random walk with drift, so a day with no prices still has a rate, a reading far from the rest counts for less, and a reset lets the rate jump. The band is its 95% interval: it widens when prices are scarce. An fg price from any day is valued at the model's rate for that day.
