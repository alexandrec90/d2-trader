Each trade of a good for runes, or of a rune for one kind of good, is a reading of its value in Ist at the Runes page's values for that day. A good's readings feed one model of its price: the line is its value each day, the band its 95% interval, a dot the median of that day's readings. Sales offering several alternatives, or paid in a mix, do not say what one good was worth and are left out.

Offers work as on the Runes page, and are fetched with the trades every {{ every }}. A rune on sale for gems, shards or keys is listed under that rune, on the Runes page. "Too close to call" means the difference is smaller than the doubt about today's values.
